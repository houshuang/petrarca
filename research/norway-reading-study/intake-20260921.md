## Known provenance limitation

The new reflection source was initially classified with the overstrong
`capture_mode: post_reading_unaided_reflection` in both its archive metadata and
registered runtime source. The opening establishes an **explicit recall reflection**;
it does not establish absence of aids, whether the book was open, or controlled
assessment conditions. The immutable original was retained, and
`provenance-correction.json` records the corrected interpretation and unknown states.

**Consumers must apply that correction; the runtime metadata field itself remains
uncorrected.** The current operator has no audited metadata-revision action. A narrow
source-correction operation is follow-up work; do not repair this with ad hoc SQL or
rewrite the immutable audio/archive. This label was not used to grade or schedule
knowledge. Current published item-level provenance conservatively says exposure
only and unresolved book/reader authorship, so it also does not represent the full
source-mode distinction. The design assessment calls for per-span evidence kinds.

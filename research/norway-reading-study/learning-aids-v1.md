# Visual grounding v1

Registered with the 13 September assessment/intake intervention, before implementation.

I dag → Se og forstå. Three on-demand references: ard/plow, åkerrein, and parallel chronology. Original diagrams label working parts or soil movement; do not present them as photographs, dated finds or reconstructions of one specimen. SVG originals and PNG outputs are in `app/assets/study`; [manifest](learning-aids-v1.json) binds source claims, rights, asset hashes and timeline intervals. Sources include SNL's specialist-authored ard account, Vestfold's field-monument guide, the Hesthag excavation report, and Universitetsmuseet i Bergen's regional period account. The book's chapter ranges are not silently converted into dated events.

The reference creates its own frozen `study_runs` snapshot. Actual explanation views, asset loads, source openings and optional feedback go through durable study events. A reference cannot grade or introduce a practice item. The original 76 curated practice items and their schedules stay intact. Reference views are additional exposure when interpreting later retrieval.

From the reference menu, “Prøv et nytt eksempel først” withholds the explanation. Åkerrein uses a separately drawn opposite-facing slope; ard asks about the soil action in a new hypothetical description. An optional recording preserves the explanation before support is revealed. This is near application, not proof of broad transfer or a matched-passage experiment. “Tydeligere” is clarity feedback, not a knowledge score. Asset-loaded events prove successful image loading, not attention or comprehension.

The timeline shares one proportional axis across regional archaeology, the geological Holocene epoch, Oslofjord farming evidence and northern early metal terminology. Northern/southern labels overlap intentionally. Holocene continues beyond the chart. Approximate dates and the later western adoption of farming are explained rather than assigned false universal boundaries.

Validation: 29 isolated study tests, TypeScript and 11 focused client checks passed. A 390×844 browser check loaded both ard diagram assets successfully from the local bundle and confirmed the reference flow. Native rendering uses bundled PNGs; no new native dependency or runtime change.
